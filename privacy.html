Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 03/08/2026

Welcome to Rarely Seen. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

1. What Data We Collect

When you visit our platform, we may collect:

  • Voluntary Data: Information you provide when creating an account, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting us (e.g., name, email address, phone number).
  • Automated Data (Cookies): We use analytics tools to act as a "digital bouncer," helping us understand how users interact with our site. This tracks metrics like button clicks, page views, and outbound links to our partner brands.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data purely to improve your experience on Rarely Seen, analyze website traffic, and, if you consent, send you updates about new homegrown brands and drops.

3. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

You have complete control over your data.

  • You have the right to request access to the data we have collected, ask us to correct inaccuracies, or request the deletion of your personal data entirely.
  • You may also opt-out of cookie tracking at any time via your browser settings.
